# Plans, SLA, and Billing
## Starter plan
Starter includes 2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM application instances, 20 GB object storage, and 50,000 API requests per calendar month. Support is email-only with a next-business-day target. Starter has no uptime SLA.
## Pro plan SLA
Pro includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA for the HTTPS edge and managed PostgreSQL primary. SLA credits are 10% of the monthly subscription for each 0.1% of downtime below 99.9%, capped at 50% of that month's subscription. Scheduled maintenance windows published 72 hours in advance do not count as downtime.
## Enterprise SLA
Enterprise includes a 99.99% monthly uptime SLA, a 15-minute Sev-1 page-out, and a named customer success manager. SLA credits follow the same 10% per 0.1% miss schedule as Pro, capped at 100% of the monthly subscription.
## Billing cycle
Subscriptions are billed monthly in arrears on the workspace anniversary date. Adding seats mid-cycle is prorated. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal. Invoices are issued as PDF to billing contacts and are due in 15 days.
