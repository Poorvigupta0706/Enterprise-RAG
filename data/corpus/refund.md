# Refunds and Cancellations
## Refund window
Monthly subscriptions may be refunded in full if cancellation is requested within 14 days of the first paid invoice and API request volume is under 10,000 for that workspace. Annual subscriptions may be refunded pro-rata within 30 days of purchase, minus any consumed overage charges.
## How to request a refund
Refunds are requested in the billing console under **Settings → Billing → Request refund**, or by emailing `billing@nimbus.example` from an owner account. The request must include the workspace id (`ws_...`) and the invoice number. Finance replies within 2 business days. Approved refunds settle to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
## Chargebacks
Opening a chargeback while a refund request is open automatically pauses the refund. Workspaces with two or more chargebacks in 12 months are ineligible for self-serve refunds and must go through Enterprise legal review.
