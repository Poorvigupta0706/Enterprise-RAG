# Support, Incidents, and Status
## Support hours
Starter email support is monitored Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 in the workspace home region's local time, excluding public holidays. Pro adds 24x5 chat. Enterprise includes 24x7 chat and phone, plus a dedicated Slack Connect channel.
## Severity levels
Sev-1 is a full production outage or data-loss event. Sev-2 is a major feature unavailable with a workaround. Sev-3 is a degraded component. Sev-4 is a question or cosmetic issue. Only Enterprise has a contractual 15-minute Sev-1 response. Pro Sev-1 target is 1 hour. Starter has no severity-based timer.
## Incident process
Public incidents are posted to `status.nimbus.example` within 15 minutes of confirmation. A preliminary RCA is shared with affected Pro and Enterprise customers in 3 business days. A final RCA is due in 10 business days. Customers must not post customer data in public status comments.
## Password reset
Users reset passwords at `https://app.nimbus.example/reset`. Reset links expire in 30 minutes and may be used once. Admins cannot read user passwords. SSO is required on Enterprise after 50 seats.
